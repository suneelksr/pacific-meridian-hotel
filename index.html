Welcome to the Pacific Meridian

Ocean views. Thoughtful service. A short walk from the sand.

The Pacific Meridian Hotel offers 48 individually designed rooms and suites, each with its own character. Located on Ocean Avenue, we are a five-minute stroll from the Santa Monica Pier and just steps from the beach. Whether you are visiting for business, a weekend escape, or a longer coastal stay, our team is here to make your time with us effortless.

Hotel Policies

A few things to know before you arrive.

Check-In / Check-Out

Check-in from 3:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out available on request.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged one night's stay.

Pets

Well-behaved pets up to 25 lbs are welcome. A one-time cleaning fee of $75 applies.

Smoking

The Pacific Meridian is a non-smoking property. A $250 cleaning fee applies to violations.

Parking

Valet parking available at $35 per night. Self-parking is not available on-site.

Children

Children of all ages welcome. Cribs and rollaway beds available on request at no additional charge.
